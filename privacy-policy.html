Privacy Policy

SmartFlow · Effective 18 November 2026 (v7)

Who we are

SmartFlow is provided by MD SHAH PORAN. For privacy questions, contact scaleroam@gmail.com. This notice describes the current Android app.

Information kept on your phone

The app saves the name you enter, your tasks and their IDs, categories, places, details, due dates, reminders, statuses, updates and form settings. It also saves the assets you add (for example name, place, holder, serial number, price, warranty and notes) and any photos or PDF documents you attach. All of this stays in app-private storage on your device. A test build may also save a local trial start and end date. You decide what to enter. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information in tasks, assets or photos unless you have a reason and permission to do so.

Camera, photos, Bluetooth and notifications

With your permission, the camera reads QR codes; those frames are processed on the device and are not saved. If you choose Take a photo, the picture is saved in the app's private storage. Photos you pick from your gallery and PDFs you pick from your files are copied into the app; the app reads only the items you select. Bluetooth is used to discover and connect to nearby supported label printers; the app uses nearby device information during that process and does not maintain a remote printer list. On older Android versions, nearby Bluetooth discovery may require location permission; the app does not use GPS to track your location. Notification permission is used for task reminders and an optional daily asset summary, which you can turn off in Settings. The app may reschedule reminders after a device restart or time change.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks for access to its private app data folder and your verified Google account email. The email is kept on this phone to avoid switching backup accounts accidentally. The first backup sends your tasks, updates, assets, attached photos and documents, and settings. Later, a changed task and its updates are uploaded in that task's file; assets, photos and settings are kept in their own files. Google stores these files under its terms. We do not operate a server or receive them. Saved changes queue an automatic backup while connected. With internet, Android sends them when able; offline changes wait for a connection. Backup can fail or be delayed. Settings offers a manual Restore from the same Google account. Restore adds missing Drive tasks without deleting or overwriting tasks already on this phone. You can stop future automatic backups in Settings. Existing Drive data stays until removed through Google's app data management.

What we receive and share

The app does not upload your tasks, assets, photos, name or QR scans to us. Google Drive receives backup data only if you enable its optional backup feature. On the Free plan the app shows Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data to show and measure ads; in the EEA, UK and similar regions you are asked for consent first and can change it from Premium > Ad privacy choices. Premium users and testers in their test period see no ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we receive only the purchase status, never your payment details. If you choose Save, Share or Android print, the selected file or information goes to the destination or app you choose, which may have its own privacy practices. If you email support, we receive the information you send and use it to answer your request.

Storage, security and deletion

Your records remain in the app's private storage. Deleting a task or asset moves it to Recycle Bin and stops its reminders. You can restore it for 30 days or permanently delete it sooner; permanent deletion also removes its photos and documents. After 30 days, expired bin items are permanently removed the next time the app is used. Uninstalling the app removes its private data. Files you exported or shared outside the app, such as PDFs, PNG labels, photos or JSON data exports, are controlled by the destination and may remain after uninstalling. A JSON export may contain tasks, updates, assets, settings and Recycle Bin items; keep it private. The app disables Android backup of its private data. Device security also depends on your phone's lock and software updates. Support emails are retained only as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

Internet, updates and plans

The app works offline. Optional Google Drive backup and restore require internet. Google Play may use the internet to install or update the app under Google's own terms. The Free plan shows ads and includes one Google Drive backup per day. SmartFlow Premium (monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase through Google Play) removes ads, allows unlimited backup and lets you attach photos and documents. Tasks, assets, export, import and restore work on every plan.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

We are the data controller for the limited information we receive, such as support emails. Data you keep only on your phone or in your own Google Drive is under your control. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract to provide the app and Premium; your consent for personalised ads (collected through Google's consent message, which you can change at any time from Premium > Ad privacy choices); our legitimate interest in answering support requests and keeping the app secure; and legal obligations, for example tax records for purchases handled by Google Play. Google acts as an independent controller or processor for AdMob, Google Play and Google Drive under its own terms.

International transfers

We operate from Malta in the European Union. Google may process ad, purchase and backup data in other countries, including the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Retention

App data stays on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app. Recycle Bin items are removed after 30 days. Google Drive backups stay until you remove them. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends, unless a longer period is required by law.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, up to 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. AdMob ads may count as sharing for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out through the privacy message shown in the app or from Premium > Ad privacy choices. Residents of California and other states with privacy laws may request access to or deletion of information we hold by emailing scaleroam@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

The app stores data in Android's app-private storage and uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections to Google. No method is completely secure. If we learn of a breach affecting information we hold, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete tasks, assets and photos and change app settings on your device. You can decline camera, Bluetooth and notification permissions, although related features will not work. If applicable data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase or object to processing of information we hold, email scaleroam@gmail.com. We cannot remotely retrieve app-private records that never leave your phone. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We may update this notice as the app changes. Material changes will be presented in the app before they apply. Effective date: 18 November 2026.
