Privacy Policy

SmartFlow Manager · Effective 17 November 2026 (v5)

Who we are

SmartFlow Manager is provided by MD SHAH PORAN. For privacy questions, contact scaleroam@gmail.com. This policy describes the current Android app.

Information kept on your phone

The app saves the technician name you enter, job names and IDs, categories, locations, descriptions, deadlines, reminders, statuses, reports and form settings in private storage on your device. A test build may also save a local trial start and end date. You decide what to enter. Avoid entering sensitive personal information unless you have a reason and permission to do so.

Camera, Bluetooth and notifications

With permission, the camera reads QR codes. Camera frames are processed on the device and are not saved as photos or sent to us. Bluetooth is used to discover and connect to nearby supported label printers. Nearby device information is used during connection and is not kept in a remote printer list. On older Android versions, Bluetooth discovery may require location permission; the app does not use GPS to track you. Notification permission is used for job reminders. The app may reschedule reminders after a device restart or time change.

What we receive and share

The app does not upload jobs, reports, technician names or QR scans to us. On the Free plan the app shows Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data to show and measure ads; in the EEA, UK and similar regions you are asked for consent first and can change it from Premium > Ad privacy choices. Premium users and testers in their test period see no ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play; we receive only the purchase status, never your payment details. If you enable optional Google Drive backup, Google receives your backup under its terms; we do not operate a backup server.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Connect Google Drive, the app requests access to its private app data folder and your verified Google account email. The email is kept on this phone to prevent accidental switching between backup accounts. The first backup sends Tracking and Preventive Work jobs and settings. Later, a changed job and its reports are uploaded in that job's file; changes to categories, statuses, forms or selected profile/PDF settings update separate settings files. Saved changes queue an automatic backup. With internet, Android sends them when able; offline changes wait for a connection. Uploads can fail or be delayed. Settings offers a manual Restore from the same Google account. Restore adds missing Drive jobs without deleting or overwriting jobs already on this phone. You can stop future automatic backups in Settings. Existing Drive data remains until removed through Google's app data management. Google controls Drive storage and availability.

Storage, security and deletion

Job records remain in the app's private storage. Deleting a job moves it and its reports to Recycle Bin and stops its reminders. You can restore it for 30 days or permanently delete it sooner. After 30 days, expired bin items are permanently removed the next time the app is used. Uninstalling removes app-private data. Files you exported or shared outside the app, such as PDFs, PNG labels or JSON data exports, are controlled by the destination and may remain after uninstalling. A JSON export may contain jobs, reports, settings and Recycle Bin items; keep it private. The app disables Android backup of its private data. Device security also depends on your phone's lock and software updates. Support emails are retained only as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations.

Internet, updates and future plans

Job management works offline. Optional Google Drive backup and restore, ads and purchases require internet. The Free plan shows ads and includes one Google Drive backup per day. SmartFlow Premium (monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase through Google Play) removes ads and allows unlimited backup. Job management, export, import and restore work on every plan.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete jobs and change app settings on your device. You can decline camera, Bluetooth and notification permissions, although related features will not work. If applicable data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, erase or object to processing of information we hold, email scaleroam@gmail.com. We cannot remotely retrieve app-private records that never leave your phone. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be presented in the app before they apply.
